`reference-slash.js` and the RGB light composition in `Surface.addLight` are adapted from [patch-gg/pixelfx](https://github.com/patch-gg/pixelfx/tree/5294518ce7c72e48b6100a744596e0acf57f2826), including its Sword slash, painter, particle simulation and seeded random generator. The original slash and particle recipes adapt [Sprite Fusion’s open-source Slash](https://www.spritefusion.com/pixel-art-vfx/slash) by Hugo Duprez. Both sources use the MIT License.

`ground-pound.js`, `ground-pound-geometry.js`, `ground-pound-particles.js` and `reference-painter.js` adapt pixelfx’s Ground pound timing, Ground slam, impact helpers, simulation and painter from the same revision. The original slam and rock recipes adapt [Sprite Fusion’s Explosion](https://www.spritefusion.com/pixel-art-vfx/explosion), and the contact star adapts [Sprite Fusion’s Impact](https://www.spritefusion.com/pixel-art-vfx/impact), both by Hugo Duprez under the MIT License.
